### Paired CUDA-event A/B (required)

Run on exclusive RTX 5090 inside `cuda/quant_mmv_test.cu`. Shape is one production FFN layer: `prompt_rows=4096`, gate/up Q4_K `17408×5120`, down Q4_K `5120×17408`. Protocol: 0 warm-ups, 3 CUDA-event replicates, arithmetic mean of the three samples, occupancy ≥ 1, zero non-finites. Time the gate/up/SwiGLU/down sequence including quantize (the quantize **is** the optimization). Write every sample to `evidence/optimization/opt025-ffn-shared-y/ffn-ab-raw.txt`.

Candidates (fixed set):

| id | Path |
|---|---|
| `baseline` | `launch_quant_mmq_mma` gate, same for up, `launch_swiglu_bf16`, `launch_quant_mmq_mma` down |
| `shared_y` | one `launch_quantize_mmq_q8_1(kQ4K)` on the FFN input, two `launch_quant_mmq_mma_y`, BF16 SwiGLU, `launch_quant_mmq_mma` down |

Winner rule: lowest arithmetic mean among candidates that launch, occupancy ≥ 1, zero non-finites, and meet the numeric gates below, **and** that mean is **strictly less** than the `baseline` mean. Equality or a slower candidate is not a win. If no optimized id strictly beats `baseline`, A/B loses: do not install production shared-Y or SwiGLU-Q8.

Numeric gates:

- Shared-Y identity on a smaller legal shape (`64×17408×5120`): one quantize + two `mma_y` is byte-identical to two independent `launch_quant_mmq_mma` on the same BF16 input (Y and both outputs).
- SwiGLU-Q8 identity on `64×5120×17408`: fused Y is byte-identical to `launch_swiglu_bf16` then `launch_quantize_mmq_q8_1(kQ4K)`. Down MMA on that Y meets ds4 Q4_K association versus CPU dequant GEMM (`ds4_q4k_association_ok`).
