## Outcome

Implement a lossless aligned device representation for Q6_K and compatible
decode/prompt consumers, targeting the 16 attention-output projections and the
full vocabulary projection. Keep only an end-to-end decode win with unchanged
full logits and P4096 behavior.

## Rationale

Quartz still spends measurable time in Q6 attention output and logits. Q6_K's
210-byte blocks force 2-byte/unaligned loads; the selected logits kernel already
uses a llama-style integer dot and two warps per row. The remaining hypothesis
is physical load efficiency, not another warp-count sweep. `ds4`'s aligned-SoA
work supports the general lossless-repack method but is not Q6 performance
evidence.

## Implementation

1. Define a lossless aligned Q6_K layout with separate aligned `d`, signed
   subscale, low-nibble and high-bit planes. Freeze exact indexing and an
   inverse byte reconstruction test for arbitrary and production rows.
2. Add aligned consumers for the selected Q6 decode MMV, attention output,
   vocabulary logits, current prompt MMQ and every fallback that may see a
   converted tensor. A kept layout replaces raw Q6 device payloads; it is never
   an additive copy.
3. Keep the existing Q8_1 activation semantics, signed 6-bit `-32` offset,
   16-value subscale boundaries, FP32 outputs and all 248,320 logits. Compare
   raw and aligned at attention-output and vocabulary shapes with rotating
   weights and report measured bytes/rate, transactions, SASS and resources.
4. Apply kernel_parity_v1 to small/pathological and production samples; compare
   every full-vocabulary logit at multiple real positions, argmax/near ties,
   NLL, graph/eager and repeated-address execution.
5. Decide on the combined Q6 contribution, not an isolated cached microkernel.
   Apply the shared decode acceptance and P4096 guard. Record load/repack time
   and 128K allocation reserve.

## Acceptance

- Lossless inverse layout, all-consumer coverage and no duplicate Q6 device
  payload are proven.
- Combined complete Q6 attention-output plus logits saves at least
  0.10 ms/token with positive paired CI; D128 and D2048 both improve.
- Full vocabulary, quality, P4096, state and memory gates pass. Rejection
  restores `integer_q8_1` raw-block dispatch.
- Add contract/fixture, runner, focused CUDA tests and
  `evidence/optimization/opt104-q6-aligned-layout/`.

## Non-goals

No vocabulary pruning, top-k-only logits, approximate argmax, Q6 re-quantizing,
extra persistent weights, repeat of the OPT-048 warp sweep or quality relaxation.
