## Outcome

## Evidence and discrepancies to resolve

- `cuda/opt137_dense_mma_decode.cuh::dense_bf16_tile_f16_mma_decode`
  calculates QK with FP32 products plus `warp_sum`, updates softmax per KV row,
  and calculates weighted V with scalar FP32 operations. `mma_qk_eight` feeds
  only `0.0F * mma_scores[0]`; there is no output-producing PV MMA. This is a
  source fact, not proof of what the compiler retains or a measured cost split.
- Pinned `.cache/authorities/llama.cpp` HEAD is
  `cc83d7b4824f73cfdda4dfbb47ee39804f71b328`. Its
  `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/fattn-mma-f16.cuh` uses MMA for both KQ and VKQ, tiled
  softmax rescaling, shared GQA columns and staged loads. Trace its actual
  D256, ncols1=1/ncols2=8 specialization and launch configuration.
- `cuda/attention_decode_path.cuh` has crossover 1024, verified maximum 4096
  and long-path threshold position 8192. Current default ranges are warp_query
  below 1024, OPT-148 on [1024,4096], warp_query on [4097,8191], OPT-137
  from 8192. Verify graph/eager callers and overrides, not just these constants.
- OPT-129's approximately 0.050 ms at 8192 is the BF16 llama-vector adapter,
  explicitly NOT native selected MMA; that sitting was non-exclusive and
  allocated prefix+1, not production capacity. OPT-136's D32768 0.243909 ratio
  is pre-137 whole-engine decode-only throughput, not a component ratio.
  Preserve historical artifacts and append corrections in the new report.
- Llama dispatch uses padded used `K.ne[1]`; Quartz uses zero-based position
  and sees position+1 tokens. Do not conflate either with allocated capacity or
  copy the number 8192 without tracing padding and masks.

Read the OPT-129/136/137/140/142/146/148 reports and raw sidecars, current
selectors and `launch_attention_prepare_partitioned`, the pinned fattn selector,
MMA and vector consumers, and `tasks/TASK-TESTING-STRATEGY.md`.
