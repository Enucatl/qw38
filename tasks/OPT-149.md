# OPT-149 — Screen normalization fused directly into Q8_1 staging

Status: done. Dependencies: OPT-135, OPT-148. Coupled IDs: none.
Priority: P1. Plan impact: none.

## Outcome

Test the deferred OPT-112 hypothesis that eligible decode residual/RMSNorm
outputs can be produced directly in the Q8_1 representation required by their
quantized consumers, avoiding the BF16 materialization and separate restaging
pass. This is a bounded screen, not a claim that Q8_1 is inherently faster.

## Candidate and boundaries

Candidate ID: norm_to_q8_1_screen_v1. Preserve exact RMSNorm math, BF16
rounding where externally observable, Q8_1 block scale/bytes, residual order,
consumer identities and invalidation. Apply only to one selected same-input
consumer group after inventory proves no BF16 or Q8Block consumer needs the
intermediate. Keep standalone BF16 and existing staging as controls. Count every
partial reduction, finalize, launch, staging and downstream projection; no
single-CTA serialization, new persistent allocation, or second encoding reuse.

Correctness covers layer kinds, mixed consumers, residual/state resets,
graph/eager equality, stale-buffer rejection and sampled full-K references.
Screen one warmup plus three rotating real-input complete decode-mixer pairs at
the selected D128/D2048 target. Screened out means no complete-family gain or
any representation/state failure. Mechanism may remain unknown; the candidate
must still have a concrete falsifying experiment.

## Acceptance

A screened-in survivor runs candidate OPT-058 quality if arithmetic/output
changes, checkpoint/cancellation and memory/reserve gates, then OPT-135
acceptance with the selected decode target and all other decode and P4096
complete-request guards. Ship only with correctness, quality, state/memory and
whole-engine evidence. Do not revive OPT-110 or claim that BF16/Q8 byte size
alone proves a win.

Deliver evidence/optimization/opt149-norm-q8/, exact encoding comparisons,
raw screen/acceptance samples and one independent family-time reconstruction.
Missing required evidence is incomplete/blocked, not no-opportunity.

## Execution requirements

Read OPT-112 and the shared testing strategy. Use explicit CUDA tiers, fixed
candidate/shape lists, one build, GPU lock and declared deadlines. No broad
profiling or simultaneous second-family candidate.
