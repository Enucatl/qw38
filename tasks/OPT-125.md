## Evidence that must be reconciled

- In `cuda/opt123_combined_stack_test.cu::run_session_arm`, the clock starts
  before prefill and decode tok/s uses outputs divided by that entire wall.
  Its long-context path uses the same function. OPT-115's request probe
  subtracts prefill. Thus 41.420→8.066 at D8192 and 22.118→1.352 at D32768
  are not evidence of a decode regression without matching boundaries.
- OPT-124 compares those Quartz rates with the pinned llama decode oracle.
  Audit that oracle's actual boundaries before reusing 0.787×/0.620×.
  Even a valid whole-engine ratio cannot identify attention's component ratio.
- `tools/opt115_pipeline_traffic.py::classify_timeline` labels gaps between
  kernel leaves as GPU idle. Missing graph children, copies, another stream,
  event perturbation or unrecorded work can occupy those gaps. Coverage based
  on filling leaf gaps is not independent evidence of hardware inactivity.
- OPT-124's D128 event busy union is 8.2922 ms but its proposed compulsory
  weight-byte minimum is 10.1829 ms. D2048 timeline components total about
  20.9921 ms, while its separate request average is 24.0193 ms. These are
  measurement/model consistency questions, not demonstrated savings.
