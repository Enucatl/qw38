### Implementation

Implemented candidate `decode_attention_qk_pv_mma_v2` in
`cuda/opt151_qk_pv_mma_decode.cuh` (new v2 path; OPT-137 kernel untouched).
Both QK and PV use `mma.m16n8k16.row.col.f32.f16.f16.f32` with PTX fragment
packing (A: K or V^T; B: Q or softmax weights; C scores/VKQ). Dense BF16 tiles
convert once to F16 via OPT-111 rounding. GQA6 padded to 8 columns; heads 6–7
masked. Llama Ampere D256 geometry: 64 threads, occupancy pin 4, nbatch_fa=64.
Frozen OPT-137 graph-bucket partitions (131/170/170). Production pin
`kSelectedOpt151QkPvMma = false`; `kSelectedOpt137DenseMma` remains `true`.

B-fragment packing was initially swapped on K vs N; mma-dep failed with
`qk_max_abs=0.0448`. After packing Q/weights as B[k=tid*2+{0,1}][n=groupID],
`qk_max_abs=3.35e-8`.
