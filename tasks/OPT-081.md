Status: pending. Dependencies: OPT-080. Coupled IDs: none.

Read [the post-080 protocol](PERFORMANCE-RECOVERY-POST-080.md).

## Outcome

A versioned Quartz `kernel_parity_v1` contract becomes the active kernel-admission
authority. Future optimization keeps must not require OPT-074 family admission
or llama-vs-FP64 production projection matching. Historical OPT-059/074 code,
pins, fixtures, and reports remain unchanged as diagnostics. No kernel change
and no performance claim.

Required policy statement in the contract, report, and ledger:

> Kernel admission proves implementation of the intended quantized operation.
> It does not prove full-model quality and does not depend on llama GPU versus
> FP64 consistency.

   `run_q4_K` / `run_q8_0` and Quartz's existing copies
   `ds4_q4k_association_ok` / `ds4_q8_association_ok` in
   `cuda/quant_mmv_test.cu`, `cuda/prompt_mmq_test.cu`, and

   - reference = independent CPU/dequant of the **same** quantized weights and
     staged/input activations;
   - classes `quantized_operation_association` and `same_math_equivalence`;
   - family table: Q8_0 `abs_scale=0.05` `rel_tol=0.05`; Q4_K and Q6_K
     `abs_scale=0.20` `rel_tol=0.05`; Q2/IQ2 `not_applicable` on this GGUF;
   - `abs_tolerance = abs_scale * sqrt(K)` with K = weight columns;
   - fail only when `abs > abs_tol AND rel > rel_tol`;
   - nonfinite count must be 0;
   - hard invariants listed in the protocol (staging typedness, Q8_1 field
     interpretation, shape/dtype/layout, selector/graph/eager mismatch);
   - `opt074_family_admission_required: false`;
   - historical CUD-001 `3e-4` and OPT-059/074 ceilings labeled
     `historical_diagnostic_only`.

   `pins/cuda_mmq_contract.json`. Do not tighten toward CUD-001. Do not derive
   scales from llama GPU or FP64 production-layer measurements.
3. Add one canonical checker: `cuda/kernel_parity.cuh` (device/host C++) and
   `tools/kernel_parity.py` (identical semantics). New work must call these.

   OPT-082. Record max abs, max rel, failing count, RMS, optional cosine.

5. Preserve `pins/production_numerics_v2_contract.json`,
   `pins/opt074_production_gpu_admission_contract.json`, and all OPT-059/074

7. Update `docs/04-numerics.md` and `docs/06-system-optimization.md` to state
