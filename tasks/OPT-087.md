# OPT-087 — Revisit other arithmetic candidates blocked primarily by numerical policy

Status: pending. Dependencies: OPT-072, OPT-082, OPT-084. Coupled IDs: none.
Priority: P1. Target: at most one extra already-implemented candidate, or none.
Read [the post-080 protocol](PERFORMANCE-RECOVERY-POST-080.md).
Quality risk: only if a candidate is actually reopened.
Instrumentation: reuse OPT-071/082; no new general backend.

## Outcome

Review OPT-072 and the ledger. Reopen only candidates whose historical outcome
was numerical-policy blocked, missing OPT-059/074 coverage, or rejected
primarily for an overly strict projection tolerance, **and** that still have
measurable already-implemented upside. Do not reopen performance losers or
structural defects. Sequence: kernel parity → model quality → performance.
No large sweep. Explicit dispositions for every candidate not reopened.

This task may conclude `no_additional_reopen=true`. That is a valid done
result.

## Implementation

1. Read `fixtures/opt072_rejection_review.json` and OPT-075–079 reports.
   Do not modify those historical files.
2. Pre-documented dispositions (confirm; do not silently reopen):

   | Candidate | Disposition | Reason |
   |---|---|---|
   | OPT-075/076 Q4 integer / late | owned by OPT-085 | not reopened here |
   | OPT-064/066 Q8/MMQ | owned by OPT-086 | not reopened here |
   | OPT-077 tiled decode GDN | do not reopen | `component_interval_not_positive`; performance, not numerical-policy |
   | OPT-078 prepared_q / veckv | do not reopen | `performance_rejected` / negative complete saving |
   | OPT-079 `kv_once` | keep | OPT-088 interaction check only |

3. If and only if the inventory finds **one** additional already-implemented
   candidate with measured upside that was blocked primarily by numerical
   policy (not owned by OPT-085/086, not a performance loser, not a hard
   error), reopen that one through OPT-082 parity, OPT-083/084 quality, and
   existing component/E2E performance. Otherwise deliver
   `no_additional_reopen=true` and the disposition table.
4. Do not search for new kernels. Do not rerun OPT-077/078 hoping the
   interval flips. Do not treat missing OPT-074 coverage of a performance
   loser as eligibility.

## Acceptance

- Fixture lists every reviewed candidate with `reopen` / `owned_elsewhere` /
  `do_not_reopen` and the evidence path.
- If a candidate is reopened, it has independent kernel/quality/performance/
  keep verdicts and must not use OPT-074 unadmitted as a blocker.
- If none is reopened, `no_additional_reopen=true` and no production pin
  change.
- Historical OPT-072 report unmodified. `claims_throughput` only if a
  reopened candidate is kept.
