## Outcome

Close the current 4097–8191 warp-query fallback and test whether llama-style
vector coverage also benefits prefixes below 1024. Reuse the kept OPT-148
consumer; this is a bounded dispatch experiment, not another attention rewrite.
Run against the actual kept or retained parent from OPT-151.

## Evidence and candidates

Read OPT-150's dispatch table, `cuda/attention_decode_path.cuh`, every caller
of `decode_attention_vec128_path_for_position`, graph bucket selection,
`cuda/opt148_short_decode_flash.cuh`, OPT-148 raw evidence, and the shared
testing strategy. Current verified_max=4096 leaves a real fallback above 4K.
Llama's padded K.ne[1] boundary is not Quartz position; retain position>=8192
long dispatch and document that exact threshold parity remains untested.

Freeze exactly two candidate dispatch rules, existing vector arithmetic and
n_parts=16 unchanged; no OPT-130 occupancy sweep:

1. `flash_vec_gap_only_v1`: OPT-148 for positions [1024,8191], warp_query
   below 1024. Target D6144 decode_only.
2. `flash_vec_all_short_v1`: OPT-148 for positions [0,8191]. Targets D512 and
   D6144 decode_only; D128 is a non-regression guard, not a mandatory gain.

Both retain the parent's long consumer and P4096 prefill. Do not increase
verified_max globally in a way that admits other kernels or unsupported shapes.
No new cache, query-preparation fusion, partition tuning or KV dtype change.
