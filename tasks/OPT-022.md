### Shared residual Y

In `cuda/full_scheduler.cu` `execute_prompt_chunk`, after mixer RMSNorm writes `prompt_normalized_`:

1. One `launch_quantize_mmq_q8_1(kQ8_0, prompt_normalized_, token_count, kResidualWidth, prompt_q8_, stream)` attributed inside the existing `qw38.mixer_mmq` CUDA-event interval.
2. GDN: `launch_q8_mmq_quality_mma` for packed_qkv, value_gate, alpha, beta from that Y. Attention: query_gate, key, value from that Y.
3. After GDN core, quantize `prompt_projected_bf16_` at K=`kGdnPackedQkvWidth` 6144 into the same `prompt_q8_` (overwrites residual Y; mixer inputs are finished) and run GDN output MMA. Attention output stays Q6_K `matrix_prompt` → `launch_quant_mmq`.

Apply the same sharing on the fused and unfused prompt paths (both already share `prompt_normalized_` at `cuda/full_scheduler.cu:2065-2106`). Do not share Y across FFN gate/up (OPT-025). Do not add a persistent Y cache keyed by pointer identity; the mixer-input quantize is explicit and once per layer.

Shared-Y identity invariant: for two Q8_0 GEMMs with identical BF16 activations, one shared quantize plus two MMA launches is byte-identical to two independent `launch_q8_mmq_quality` launches (deterministic D4 quantize). Component test must assert that.
