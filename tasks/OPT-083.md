# OPT-083 — Q4 production admission and winner selection under v3

Status: pending. Dependencies: OPT-081, OPT-082, OPT-073, OPT-075, OPT-076.
Coupled IDs: none.
Priority: P1. Target: at most one production Q4 decode/FFN survivor.
Read [the post-080 protocol](PERFORMANCE-RECOVERY-POST-080.md).
Quality risk: changed Q4 reduction/association; Q8Block FP32 staging stays
typed. OPT-075/076 `retain_packed` remains historically correct; this task
is the successor decision.

## Outcome

Reopen the existing Q4 candidates under **direct Quartz-vs-llama** v3
admission. Do not treat OPT-075/076 as historical numerical rejections;
their recorded blocker was missing OPT-074 v2 coverage.

Candidates (exactly these three; no new fusion/tile/compiler/half-scale grid):

1. Shipping control: packed / `paired_staged`
2. OPT-075 cooperative integer Q4 paired (`integer_q8` + `paired_integer`)
3. OPT-076 late-reduction survivor `late_w4` (`integer_q8_late`, 4 warps/row)

Do not automatically install OPT-075 before evaluating OPT-076 if OPT-076
subsumes it. Screen all three; at most one survivor is eligible for
production keep. Historical screens (paired ~16.43→11.77 ms; `late_w4`
~16.42→9.82 ms complete FFN) may rank, but a keep requires fresh v3
numerics, dispatch proof, quality, and acceptance-tier performance.

## Implementation

1. Require OPT-082 `candidate_testable=true` for Q4 gate/up and down.
   Missing coverage is unresolved admission, not a numerical rejection.
2. For each candidate, prove actual eager and captured production launches
   (not header-only selectors). Recapture graphs after the override.
   Preserve Q8/Q6 selectors and OPT-058 staging invalidation.
3. Compare candidate outputs to pinned llama GPU on all frozen calibration
   and held-out Q4 gate/up/down cases using OPT-081 limits. Held-out cannot
   enlarge those limits. Record `candidate_v3_admitted` independently of
   llama-vs-FP64 drift.
4. Enforce staging/layout/finite/integer-sum hard rules. A `sum_x` producer
   must not feed a `sum_q` consumer.
5. After local admission, run engine-level llama differential on the
   surviving arithmetic (hidden/logits/top-1, reset/replay) with limits
   frozen in this task's contract before candidate outputs.
6. Run OPT-073 engine non-regression plus PPL ≤1.01 and recurrence
   incremental NLL ≤0.02. Keep `model_quality_absolute` visible; it may
   set `release_blocked` without forcing `production_kept=false` by itself
   when engine non-regression and PPL/recurrence pass.
7. Complete 64-layer rotating FFN component acceptance (3+10) of control vs
   one survivor, then five D2048+32 engine pairs. Cross-workload P4096
   guard as in the post-069 protocol.
8. Final status must be one of: numerical rejection, quality blocked,
   performance rejected, or production kept. Shipping stays packed until a
   keep actually pins a new selector.
