# OPT-113 — Freeze and measure the coupled-stack recovery combination

Status: pending. Dependencies: OPT-107, OPT-108, OPT-109, OPT-110, OPT-111,
OPT-114. Coupled IDs: none. Primary target: original comparative outcome gates.

## Outcome

Freeze the exact production survivors from OPT-107 through OPT-112 (omitting
OPT-112 when its explicitly deferred verdict has no survivor) and measure them
against the authenticated OPT-106 production control and pinned llama.cpp in
one exclusive-GPU sitting. Report internal improvement, parity, and the
unchanged OPT-056 +5% outcome independently.

## Freeze rules

1. Include only configurations whose owning task completed its required kernel
   parity, full-model quality, state, memory, complete-component, and end-to-end
   keep gates. A rejected diagnostic path must not leak into production.
2. Preserve admitted vertical slices as units. Do not silently select an
   unmeasured subset of prepared query, KV representation, fast math,
   persistent state, or source-adapter code.
3. Authenticate source/build/container, GGUF, pinned llama revision, compiler
   flags by translation unit, effective selectors, layout/type versions, graph
   identities, persistent allocations, and copied-source license/provenance.
4. Use `post106_control` and one `post113_selected`. If no candidate survives,
   selected equals control and Quartz is measured once.
5. Treat OPT-112 as optional for this sitting. If it has not been admitted, a
   documented deferred verdict is sufficient; it must not be silently included
   as a candidate or block OPT-113.

## Verification and evidence

- Add batch/iteration contracts, fixture, runner, host validators, native
  selector/freeze test, and
  `evidence/optimization/opt113-coupled-stack-gate/REPORT.md`.
- Run the complete current quality suite before long timing, including
  candidate NLL, per-case deltas, recurrence, deterministic continuation,
  nonfinites, and the inherited absolute task-quality verdict kept separate
  from engine regression.
- In one serialized sitting run fresh P4096, D128, and D2048 samples for
  control, selected, and pinned llama with established counts/order. Report raw
  latency, tok/s, paired deltas/confidence intervals, p50, and p95. Do not reuse
  OPT-106 llama numbers.
- Rerun the frozen 2K gate, 128K allocation ledger, prompt-to-decode handoff,
  graph/eager equality, checkpoint/restore, cancellation/frontier, divergent
  prefix replay, and state-format round trips.
- Reconcile the 128K `memory_fit` result against the actual allocation
  inventory before declaring the gate failed. A small accounting discrepancy
  must be fixed in the ledger or explained; do not raise a cap without an
  allocation-backed reason.
- Publish matched attribution for the selected build only as diagnostic,
  non-additive evidence. Do not turn family event sums into a wall-time claim.

## Acceptance

- `internal_improvement_with_quality`: positive paired geometric throughput
  result over P4096/D128/D2048, with every workload meeting the existing
  non-regression bound and all quality/state/memory gates passing.
- `llama_parity`: Quartz throughput at least fresh pinned llama on every
  workload.
- `opt056_plus5`: Quartz at least 1.05× pinned llama on every workload, decode
  p95 no worse, full quality pass, and OPT-016 2K pass.
- Marking OPT-113 done means the complete sitting and honest independent
  verdicts are delivered. It does not imply that any comparative gate passed.

## Non-goals

No new optimization inside the gate, selector retuning, quality concession,
benchmark change, historical fixture rewrite, extra sampling after an
inconclusive result, or release claim from diagnostic attribution.
