This chapter is the human-readable coverage record for the documentation gate. An implemented concept is a non-educational ledger task with checked-in code, contract, fixture, test, or operational evidence. Each row names the handbook location, preserves one of the canonical claim labels, states the invariant/failure or proof boundary, and links repository evidence. **Measured** means retained artifact/device evidence; **External** means a linked authority; **Estimated** means reproducible arithmetic; **Proposed** means design or unevidenced future work. Historical and partial results remain visibly qualified.

Educational tasks are covered by their existing chapter links in the implementation ledger and are not duplicated here. Active or blocked rows are included to prevent partial work being mistaken for admission.
